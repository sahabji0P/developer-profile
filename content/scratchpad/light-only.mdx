---
title: Light-only on purpose
date: "2026-09-04"
description: Why this portfolio stays on a cream ground with no dark theme toggle.
icon: Sun
---

The site forces a light cream ground (`#f7f7f4`) and sets `color-scheme: light` on the root. There is no dark-mode toggle and no `prefers-color-scheme` swap.

Garamond body type and the orange accent were tuned for that ground. A second theme would mean re-checking contrast on every prose element; for a small portfolio I would rather ship one deliberate look.
