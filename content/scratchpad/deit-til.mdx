Training a DeiT-small on an already-augmented MRI set, the surprising bit was not the headline accuracy — it was how little custom architecture we needed once the ImageNet-pretrained transformer had a clean four-class head.

The paper path (ICCCNT / NeuroVision) is written up longer under Writing; this note is the reminder I keep: start from the pretrained patch model, then spend the budget on folds and class balance, not on inventing a new backbone.
