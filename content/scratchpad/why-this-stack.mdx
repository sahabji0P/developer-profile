date: "2026-09-10"
description: Next App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind v4, and MDX for a small reading site.

---

I keep the stack narrow on purpose: Next.js App Router for routes and metadata, TypeScript for the content loaders, Tailwind v4 for tokens and a few layout utilities, and MDX for posts that stay editable as files.

Nothing here needs a CMS or a client data layer. `gray-matter` + `next-mdx-remote` is enough to turn folders under `content/` into sorted indexes and detail pages.
