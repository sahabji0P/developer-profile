I presented **Brain Tumor Classification Using DeiT Vision Transformer** at the [16th ICCCNT](https://16icccnt.com/) [IEEE](https://www.ieee.org) Conference at IIT Indore in July 2025. The paper is by Shashwat Jain, Nikhil, and Rudraksh Bhardwaj, with co-authors Dr. Gitika Sharma and Dr. Himanshu.

Brain Tumors are among the most complicated and serious neurological disorders due to their unpredictable structure, uncontrollable growth patterns of brain cells and several subtypes - over 120 classified by World Health Organization. Prior and accurate detection is critical for immediate treatment and significantly influences patient prognosis and survival rates. However, manual examination using medical imaging is time consuming, subject to human error, and inconsistent, potentially resulting in delayed or incorrect assessments. This paper explores advanced automated approaches for brain tumor classification that leverage recent developments in transformer based DeiT models to enhance diagnostic accuracy and reliability. We demonstrate a novel approach using Vision Transformers, specifically a DeiT (Data-efficient Image Transformers) architecture for classifying the types of brain tumors. Our model depends on the deit-small-patch16-224 variant, which has been pre-trained on ImageNet and fine-tuned for tumor classification tasks.

The classifier uses *DeiT-small-patch16-224*, pre-trained on ImageNet and fine-tuned for this task. Five-fold cross-validation on the augmented MRI set:

- 99.93% average accuracy
- 34,272 images derived from 7,022 originals
- four classes: glioma, meningioma, pituitary, and no tumor

The associated project is [NeuroVision](https://github.com/sahabji0P/NeuroVision), an end-to-end full-stack application using a Vision Transformer for real-time MRI tumor detection.
